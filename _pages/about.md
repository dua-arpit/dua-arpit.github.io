I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at Virginia Tech (since December 2023) and a Principal Quantum Scientist at [QuEra Computing](https://www.quera.com/) (since August 2025). At QuEra, I lead the collaboration with NVIDIA and manage QuEra's work in the Quantum Benchmarking Initiative (QBI). Before these positions, I was a Joint [Simons](https://projects.iq.harvard.edu/ultra-qm/home)-[IQIM](https://iqim.caltech.edu/) postdoc at Caltech under Xie Chen and a PhD student at Yale University under Meng Cheng and Liang Jiang.

My group researches quantum error correction, fault-tolerant quantum computation, and topological order. Current directions include:

- High-rate quantum LDPC codes, such as bivariate bicycle, lifted-product, and GALA/Kasai codes, and their implementation on reconfigurable neutral-atom arrays.
- Logical operations on these codes: code surgery and lattice surgery, transversal gates, and magic-state preparation.
- Fault-tolerant protocols from dynamical and Floquet codes, spacetime concatenation, and decoders.
- Fracton and fermionic stabilizer codes, subsystem symmetries, and topological phases as a source of new codes.
- Fault-tolerant architectures and resource estimates for cryptographically relevant workloads.
- Code design with machine-learning and AI-assisted methods.

Our research is supported by a DOE Office of Science grant (lead PI, 2026 to 2029) and an NSF Physics (PHY) grant (PI). I am also a Co-Investigator on a Yale-led NSF Quantum Leap Challenge Institute and on the DOE Genesis Mission Phase I project on AI-enabled co-design of fault-tolerant logical interfaces in qLDPC codes.

Email me at adua@vt.edu (Virginia Tech) or adua@quera.com (QuEra) to learn more about my research and collaboration.

  • Pedro Medina

    Graduate student

    Pedro is a Ph.D. student in Physics at Virginia Tech. He is interested in the construction and properties of qLDPC codes, their performance under different noise models, and the development of decoding strategies. His current research focuses on single-shot decoding and understanding the scaling behavior of logical error rates in qLDPC codes.

  • Chuen Hei Chan

    Graduate student

    Chuen Hei is a Ph.D. student in Physics and is interested in topological order in quantum information and the algebraic structure of qLDPC codes. His current work focuses on designing and optimizing qLDPC codes to reduce physical-qubit overhead while preserving logical capacity and code distance. He has also been working with Prof. Sophia Economou and Prof. Edwin Barnes on quantum network and photonic graph state generation, and his broader research interests include the interplay between quantum error correction, quantum networks, and distributed quantum computing.

  • Alexander Shupp

    Alexander completed his BS in Physics at Virginia Tech in May 2026. His research interests include quantum error correction, graphical tensor calculi, and topological quantum computing.

I am a Principal Quantum Scientist at QuEra Computing and an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at Virginia Tech. Before this, I was a Joint Simons–IQIM postdoc at Caltech with Xie Chen and a PhD student at Yale University with Meng Cheng and Liang Jiang.

My group at Virginia Tech works on quantum error correction, fault-tolerant quantum computation, and topological order.

Research

  • High-rate qLDPC codes

    Bivariate bicycle, lifted-product, and GALA/Kasai codes for reconfigurable neutral-atom arrays.

  • Logical operations

    Code and lattice surgery, transversal gates, and magic-state preparation.

  • Fault-tolerant protocols

    Dynamical and Floquet codes, spacetime concatenation, and decoders.

  • Topological order

    Fracton and fermionic stabilizer codes, subsystem symmetries, and topological phases as a source of codes.

  • Architectures

    Fault-tolerant architectures and resource estimates for cryptographically relevant workloads, with AI-assisted co-design.
